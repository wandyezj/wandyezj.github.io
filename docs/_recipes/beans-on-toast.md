# Beans on Toast

Create bean spread for beads on toast

## Ingredients

- Instant Pot Black Beans (see recipe) drained of water
- 3 cloves mild garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- Olive Oil

## Equipment

- Chefs Knife
- Deep Pan
- measuring spoons
- Masher

## Directions

1. Peal and mince garlic
1. preheat pan on low
1. Place a quarter cup of olice oil in pan and heat
1. Place minced garlic in olive oil
1. Gently simmer garlic for about 2 minutes (do not overdo it)
1. Place beans in pan and stir together until warm
1. Add another quarter cup of olive oil
1. Add salt
1. mash beans to desired texture
1. Cook off excess water until desired thickness is reached
1. let cool
1. Place in jars for storage, refrigerate or freeze


Making Toast

1. Toast sourdough until lightly brown
1. Spread on beans
1. Drizzle olive oil over beans
1. Top with light sprinkling of maldon salt
1. Enjoy!
