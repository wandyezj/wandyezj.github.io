# Instant Pot Black Beans

Cooking Dried black beans in the instant pot

## Ingredients

- 1 lb dried bean (Rancho Gordo preferred)
- 7 cups water (room temperature)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 Bay Leaves

## Equipment

- instant pot
- scale
- measuring cup
- measuring spoons

## Directions

1. Rinse instant pot
1. sort beans check for rocks (rocks are not tasty and are bad for your teeth)
1. rinse beans
1. place beans in pot
1. place water, salt, and bay leaves in pot
1. put lid on pot
1. set to high pressure
1. set to 30 minutes, natural release for 15 minutes
1. let the beans stay warm
1. For storage - transfer beans to jars and store in fridge or freezer
1. Microwave to reheat
